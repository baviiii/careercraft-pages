# Product names

Velyo is crowded and generic. This list is for the **app people open** (résumés, tracker, interview, coach, AU jobs, insights, news). **Horus stays the engine.**

The product is two rooms: **Craft** (make the file) and **Market** (read the ground). A good name sounds like a workshop + a next move — not “CareerSomething.”

Line that works under any of these: *A workshop for the next move.*

Domains were **not** reserved. Check Porkbun / Cloudflare before you fall in love. Cheap usually means `.app` `.so` `.dev` `.studio` `.work` or a free `.com` on a weird spelling. Pricey means a real English word whose `.com` is taken or aftermarket.

## Do not use

- Anything with **Career / Job / Resume / Path / Craft / Copilot / Hive / Nest / Pulse**
- **Horus** as the consumer name (keep it as the engine)
- **Kaurna** or sacred language without a real partnership
- **Quillo** (AI notes app), **Velora**, **Plynt**, **Lumo / Lumio / Liora**, **Nexora / Nexo / Nexi**
- **Bricolo** (inside joke on the typeface; Brico is fintech)

---

## How to shop later

1. Pick 8 from **Cheap** and 3 from **Mid**.
2. Search each on Porkbun or Cloudflare (`.com` `.com.au` `.app` `.so`).
3. If `.com` is ~$12 unused, take it. If it is aftermarket money, walk.
4. Say it once to someone. If they spell it wrong, use *their* spelling.
5. Quick ASIC + a Google of `"Name" software` before you print it on the sidebar.
